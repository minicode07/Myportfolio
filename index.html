Internship & Analytics Case Study

HR Research Intern @ Reliance Industries Limited — Gadimoga, Kakinada

Project: Attrition & Employee Motivation Analysis

120

Survey respondents
from a 200 candidate pool

17

Data tables & charts created

Primary attrition drivers

40% Career Growth · 36% Family Factors · 16% Compensation

Actionable retention recommendations

Findings were presented to management as a retention playbook: prioritize structured career-pathing and internal mobility to address the 40% growth-driven risk; introduce family-support policies and scheduling flexibility for the 36% family-factor cohort; and treat compensation as a targeted lever (16%), not the sole retention strategy. Combined with clearer role design and engagement tracking, these actions convert survey insight into a practical workforce-stability plan.
